Public sanitized evidence repository mapping the Jedha Data Science & AI RNCP Level 6 six-block track to technical artifacts: data infrastructure, EDA, ML, NLP, MLOps and project governance.

---

## What this repo proves

| Evidence | Signal |
|---|---|
| Six-block structure | ability to organize certification evidence cleanly |

| Model/data governance templates | regulated-data maturity |
| Links to executable repos | avoids isolated notebooks and favors production-oriented proof |

---

## Recruiter-readable message

> This repository organizes sanitized technical evidence for a Data Science & AI training path. It avoids private school documents and focuses on public technical artifacts: architecture, SQL, code, analysis, ML evaluation, MLOps, model cards, data cards and project governance.

---

## Role relevance

| Role family | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Data Engineer | data infrastructure, SQL, ingestion |

## What this repo is not
