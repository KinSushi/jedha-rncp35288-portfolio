# AIA Bloc 1 — Data & AI Governance

## Purpose

This folder organizes public, sanitized evidence for the governance block of the AI Architect track (RNCP38777, renewed as RNCP41993): how an organization governs its data and AI systems — roles, standards, access, regulation and continuous improvement.

## Technical skills

- data and AI asset mapping: sources, flows, sensitivity levels, AI systems inventory
- risk analysis: security, bias, confidentiality, usage; prioritization of compliance work
- roles and responsibilities: data owners, data stewards, RACI matrix, business/technical coordination
- quality and traceability standards: data contracts, data catalog, lineage, interoperability
- access and protection policy: RBAC / ABAC, pseudonymization and anonymization
- regulation and ethics by design: GDPR, EU AI Act, ISO standards, incident handling
- inclusive awareness programme (accessibility for people with disabilities)
- governance KPIs, audits, improvement plans and regulatory/technology watch
